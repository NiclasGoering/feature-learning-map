one_line: "On the random hierarchy model, Bayes-optimal denoising by belief propagation has a class-reconstruction phase transition at $sf=1$ while low-level features change smoothly, matching ImageNet diffusion."
summary: "The paper studies noising a datum for a time $t$ and then denoising it, in ImageNet diffusion models and in the random hierarchy model (RHM) of tree-structured data. For the RHM, Bayes-optimal denoising is exact by belief propagation on the tree. A mean-field (annealed) map predicts that, for $sf<1$ and infinite depth, the root class is recovered only below a critical noise, while low-level latent variables degrade smoothly. After the transition the class has changed but low-level features of the original datum can persist. The same ordering of similarity curves is seen in ConvNeXt activations on ImageNet and in a CNN trained on the RHM."
