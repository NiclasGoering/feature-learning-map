- Read the HTML of v3 through Sec. 4 (setting, Thm 1, Cor 1, Thm 2, Remark 4) and the headings of Sec. 5-6 and the appendices; proofs not checked. Display equations (3.3)-(3.6) were read from a text extraction that dropped some case-by-case pieces, so the exact regimes of validity should be checked in the PDF.
- The student has quadratic activation with weights $W\in\mathbb R^{d\times r_s}$ and no trainable second layer; the norms $\|w_j\|^2$ play the role of second-layer weights (homogeneous parametrisation). Thm 2 trains the directions on the Stiefel manifold, then fits the radial part by a cheap fine-tuning step, so `learned_layers` is `first-layer-only` for the proved SGD result; the continuous-time analysis and simulation train the full weight matrix (overrides on r1, r2, r4).
- The paper cites Ren et al. (2504.19983) only in its conclusion, so no relation is recorded.
- Label noise is sub-Gaussian (`other`). Fig. 1(b) uses Euclidean SGD, which the paper only conjectures to share the Stiefel scaling.
