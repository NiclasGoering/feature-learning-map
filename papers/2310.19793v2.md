summary: "The paper studies population gradient flow for multi-index regression with Gaussian inputs in dimension $d$. The link function is learned non-parametrically infinitely faster than the projection, which turns the loss into a function of a subspace correlation matrix. This loss has a unique global minimiser and all saddles lie on the boundary of the set of PSD matrices. Gradient flow from a random start visits a flag of subspaces set by the Hermite decomposition of the target and escapes the $k$-th saddle after time of order $d^{s_k-1}$, with $s_k$ the relative information exponents. For a known link, gradient flow on the Stiefel manifold can instead be trapped at bad optima with high probability."

- The paper has no experiments beyond cartoons; the figures are illustrations.
