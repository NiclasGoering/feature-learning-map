- Read the PDF of v3 (13 May 2025; text extracted) for Sec. I to VI except the appendices and the details of the replica derivation in Sec. II. The arXiv HTML page for v3 did not load (HTTP 429 on repeated tries), so `html: not-available` and anchors are `none`.
- This is a theory of kernel (GP) regression, not of feature learning: the kernel is fixed. The authors say that studying such flows "may improve our understanding of feature learning", so it is entered as T0, and `moves_mean: no` throughout.
- Data averaging uses the grand-canonical trick (Poisson-distributed dataset size with mean $\eta$) and the replica trick; the approximation is a first-order expansion in the variance $\delta c$ of integrated-out shells, hence `solver: perturbative` and `approximations: other`.
- The non-Gaussian example is deliberately a toy (one integrated mode, small effect); the paper itself calls the effect small. It is the paper's only check of the input-dependent ridge.
- The scaling-law comparison (Fig. 1) uses NTK regression on four binary tasks with Gaussian feature histograms checked in an appendix; the exponent matches earlier results obtained from self-consistent equations, and the paper notes the leading-order result sits at the threshold of the equivalent-kernel approximation.
