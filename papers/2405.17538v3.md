summary: "The paper joins the neural-network field-theory (NNFT) correspondence, which turns a network and its parameter law into a field theory, with Bayesian renormalisation, which coarse-grains parameters by their Fisher information. Parameters whose diagonal Fisher entry at the trained estimate lies below a cutoff $\\Lambda$ are marginalised and reset to a prior, giving parameter laws and, through NNFT, actions $S_\\Lambda$ that the paper reads as an information-theoretic RG flow. For infinite-width networks of any depth with frozen hidden layers and a Gaussian last-layer posterior, the flowing predictive is a Gaussian process with renormalised mean and covariance. For a single-hidden-layer cos-net this flow rescales the propagator of a free scalar field. A numerical ensemble of gradient-descent-trained ReLU networks shows the loss unchanged until a critical cutoff."

- RG record: Sense (c), neural-network field theory with an information-theoretic Bayesian RG: the parameters of a trained network with Fisher diagonal $\lambda_A<\Lambda$ at the MAP estimate ("sloppy", Eq. (53)) are integrated out and reset to a prior (Eqs. (54), (74)–(75), (96)). What flows is the dual action $S_\Lambda=S^{\rm free}_\Lambda+S^{\rm int}_\Lambda$ through renormalised cumulants (Eqs. (56)–(58)), which is defined for any architecture but has no flow or beta equation; it is computed only for the free infinite-width theory with a Gaussian last-layer posterior (Eq. (69)), as mean and covariance in Eq. (76) and, for the cos-net, as one scalar $\sigma_w(\Lambda)^2$ multiplying the propagator (Eqs. (86), (87), (89)). The order is exact within that Gaussian ansatz but zeroth order in interactions: no $g^{(n\ge3)}_\Lambda$, no $1/N$ correction, no loops, so nothing beyond first order; maturity is a definition plus one analytic example plus a small numerical check (Figs. 9–11), and the momentum-shell ERG reading (Eq. (90)) is conditional on $\sigma_w(\Lambda)^2$ increasing, which the paper does not show.
- Read the full arXiv HTML of v3 (Sections 1–5, all numbered equations, Figs. 1–11 and captions); the references were only skimmed and there is no appendix. v3 is the SciPost version (SciPost Phys. Core 8, 027). The year is 2024 (first version). Grades are `none` throughout because the formulas need the trained posterior (mean, covariance, Fisher metric) as input and do not predict a quantity from data statistics.
- Judgement calls: r1 is placed in `finite-exact` because the Sec. 3 construction is stated to hold for any architecture and width, but it is a definition and the paper computes nothing at finite width. r2 and r3 are `bayes` (posterior hyperparameters, Gaussian ansatz (69) peaked at the gradient-descent estimate). The numerics (r4) train an ensemble with plain gradient descent from a Gaussian prior; the paper treats it as posterior samples (Secs. 2.2, 4.2.1) without testing agreement with an NNGP posterior, so r4 is `gd` with `gd-simulation`. The paper does not say whether the hidden layers are trained in Sec. 4.2; the Fisher metric and resampling run over the whole tuple $\theta_j$, so `learned_layers: [all]` is my reading.
- Inconsistencies in the paper: footnote 3 calls BRG a non-perturbative exact RG, while Sec. 3.3 calls the information-shell scheme a perturbative form of the exact scheme of Ref. [31] (a convection–diffusion equation, footnote 17, which is not used). The statement that BRG "corresponds exactly" to momentum-shell ERG rests on the cos-net example, Eq. (90) and a conditional ("If $\sigma_w(\Lambda)^2$ increases under the RG flow"). Sec. 4.2.1 gives last-layer width 2000 and the table in Fig. 7 gives 2048.
- Vocab gap: `setting.activation`, wanted cosine ("generalized cos-net"), used `other` (r3). `dials.closure`, wanted a value for a construction with no single-site closure (r1), used `none`; Gaussian last-layer posterior ansatz recorded as `gaussian` (r2, r3). `method.approximations`, wanted "diagonal-Fisher information-shell truncation of exact BRG" (r1), used `other`. `method.method`, wanted field-theory / Edgeworth cumulant reconstruction (NNFT), used `information-theoretic` plus `gp-kernel-limit`. `quantity`, wanted renormalised action or couplings of the dual field theory, used `posterior-predictive` and `kernel-representation`. `mechanism.label`, wanted "coarse graining resets low-Fisher parameters to the prior (inverse of learning)", used `not-analysed` (r1, r4) and `kernel-fixed-at-init` (r2). `learning_rule`, no value for a gradient-descent ensemble read as posterior samples (split between r2/r3 and r4 as above). `width_limit` for a definition valid at any width, used `finite-exact` (r1).
