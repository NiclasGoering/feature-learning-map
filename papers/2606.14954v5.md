summary: "The paper builds a general framework for representation costs of parameter-space regularisers: the cost of a function is the least regulariser value that realises it, with a native function space, existence results, and conditions for parameter and function problems to share minimisers. It recovers kernel methods, wavelets and Besov spaces, and shallow networks with variation spaces as special cases. For depth-$L$ ReLU networks with weight decay it shows the cost is the $L/2$ power of a $(2/L)$-seminorm and the native space is quasi-Banach, with a nonconvex unit ball when $L>2$. A representer theorem bounds the width needed, and native spaces nest as depth grows."
