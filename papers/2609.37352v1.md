one_line: "A graph of chains joined when their pairwise R-hat is small recovers which chains share a posterior mode, separating multimodality from poor mixing."
summary: "Standard R-hat treats chains that settle in different posterior modes like chains that fail to mix. The paper builds a graph whose nodes are chains and whose edges join pairs with pairwise R-hat below a threshold. As the run length grows, its connected components partition chains by the mode they explored; with no isolated chain their number counts the modes. At least two chains per mode are needed. It is tested on bimodal Gaussians, Cauchy regression on two-line data and a pharmacokinetic model with label switching, using Stan with eight chains."
