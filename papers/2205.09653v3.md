one_line: "Dynamical mean field theory of gradient flow in infinite-width mean-field networks, with kernel order parameters and a Monte Carlo solver bridging lazy and rich training."
summary: "The paper derives a path-integral dynamical field theory of gradient flow for wide networks with output scale $\\gamma=\\gamma_0\\sqrt N$ at fixed $P$ and training time. Feature and gradient kernels at pairs of times are deterministic order parameters fixed by saddle-point equations, which also give the NTK and the predictions. At $\\gamma_0=1$ the stochastic process equals the Tensor Programs one. Deep linear networks give algebraic matrix equations, nonlinear networks an alternating sampling solver. Weight decay yields a kernel regressor with the final NTK, and a comparison shows that the gradient-independence ansatz and low-order perturbation theory fail at large $\\gamma_0$."
