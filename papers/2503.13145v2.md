summary: "The paper treats a network as a molecular system whose parameters are coordinates and whose loss is the energy, and samples the entropy as a function of train loss and test accuracy (or test loss) with Wang-Landau Monte Carlo and Wang-Landau molecular dynamics. Four tasks are studied: a spiral classification, house prices, a reduced MNIST and CIFAR-10, and a polymer language-model head, with up to about a million parameters. At equal train loss, the equilibrium (max-entropy) states have equal or higher test accuracy, or equal or lower test loss, than SGD-trained states. The advantage is larger for narrower networks and vanishes at width 1000 on the spiral regression task."

- Read the main text and Methods of the HTML version; the entry check then read the Supplementary Materials in the arXiv PDF (MD check, datasets, Tables 1-6, CIFAR-10 ResNet, Figs. 7-9), which confirm the trends; the MNIST width trend is weak (3 and 4 channels alike).
- The comparison point is the entropy-weighted mean accuracy (Eq. 1) for the WLMC spiral task and the max-entropy point at each train loss for the WLMD tasks. The SGD baseline is the mean over runs in the main text but the lowest test loss per train-loss bin in the SM.
- `learning_rule: [bayes, gd]` is a judgement call: the max-entropy state is the equilibrium of a flat measure on a bounded parameter box, conditioned on train loss, and it is compared with SGD (Adam for the width study). No likelihood or temperature is defined, so the dial `temperature` is `not-stated`.
- No scaling limit is analysed: the networks are finite and the evidence is numerical. The validator requires a regime tag for any paper with `bayes`, so R6 (classical thermodynamic, entropy of the weight space at fixed data) and `high-dim` were chosen as the closest fit; this is a forced classification and should be checked.
- Parameters are confined to $[-2/\sqrt{W},2/\sqrt{W}]$, so the entropy depends on that range; the paper says the range does not change results qualitatively but shows no data in the main text. Absolute entropy is defined only up to a constant.
- The polymer task fine-tunes only a regressor head on frozen transformer embeddings, so it is not a full transformer. Test accuracy for WLMD is a smoothed sigmoid surrogate (Eq. 2).
