- Read the abstract, introduction, Secs. 2 to 7 and the figure captions; appendices were skimmed, not read in full. The paper is mainly empirical. Sec. 6 is explicitly heuristic ("do not have the status of mathematical proofs"), hence `phys` for r3.
- Main-text setting: fully-connected networks with softplus activation and a soft-hinge loss, gradient flow discretised with momentum; ReLU and CNN variants appear in Sec. 7 and the appendices. `activation: [relu, other]` records this.
- The text gives $a\approx1.3$ measured in Fig. 5 while the abstract says $a<2$ with $a\to2$ as depth grows; the heuristic formula $a=2/(1+1/L)$ gives $1.66$ at $L=2$, which the paper calls consistent with its Eq. (18). The two numbers are not the same, so a reader should check this.
- The problem is posed as lazy against feature regime at $P$ fixed, so `inf-meanfield-fixedP` is the closest group for the feature side. The paper itself identifies $\alpha\sim h^{-1/2}$ with the mean-field scaling.
- r4 label `not-analysed`: the paper states no feature-level explanation for the architecture dependence.
