summary: "The paper considers deep fully connected Bayesian networks with Gaussian weight priors, a Gaussian likelihood, and a single variance shared by the last hidden layer and the likelihood, which gets an Inverse-Gamma prior. It proves that the marginal posterior on any finite input set is within Wasserstein-1 distance $c/\\sqrt{n_{\\min}}$ of the posterior of the corresponding NNGP mixture. For a scalar output, the limit is a multivariate Student-t with $2a+k$ degrees of freedom. The proof couples the conditional-on-variance bounds through a convexity property of $\\mathcal W_1$. NUTS-Gibbs simulations with erf networks of width up to 128 illustrate the convergence."

- Read Secs. 1-4 in full and the dependency structure of the proof; the appendix proofs (Appendices A-D) were not checked.
- The variance $\sigma^2$ is shared by the last hidden layer's weights and biases and the Gaussian likelihood (eq. 8, Thm 3.4); that choice makes the posterior mean independent of $\sigma^2$, so the Student-t limit has the NNGP mean (my reading of eqs. 9-11).
- Thm 3.4 constrains $b>(1+\frac{\varepsilon+2}{2\varepsilon+2})\|y\|_F^2$ with $\varepsilon<1/\|K'\|_{op}$ and $n_L=1$ is assumed for the Student-t statement.
- Simulations use `NUTS` for the weights and a generalised inverse Gaussian draw for the variance; no R-hat or ESS is reported, hence `mixing: low` is a judgement.
