summary: "The paper considers the Gibbs measure $e^{-H/\\varepsilon}$ of a smooth Hamiltonian with several local minima, at low temperature $\\varepsilon$. It splits the measure into metastable regions around the minima, shows local Poincaré and log-Sobolev constants that scale well in $\\varepsilon$, and bounds the mean-difference between regions by a weighted transport distance. Together these give the Eyring-Kramers formula for the Poincaré constant, and for the first time for the log-Sobolev constant, which differs from it by a factor from the logarithmic mean of the partition sums unless the minima are degenerate. The constants are exponentially large in the energy barrier between the two deepest minima."

- Read Secs. 1-2 (setting, assumptions, outline, all main results) of v4 from the HTML; the proofs in Secs. 3-4 and the appendices were not read. The venue is taken from the journal reference in the arXiv record.
- This is general diffusion theory on $\mathbb{R}^n$ with no neural network. It is listed because it supplies the barrier-dependent mixing constants cited in open problem op-03. Setting fields are `not-stated`, and `depth: one-hidden-layer` is a placeholder, as in other generic-sampling entries.
- Regime tag R6 is a judgement call (a "classical thermodynamic" Gibbs measure), and `high-dim` is put on r2 only because the prefactors of Cor. 2.15 depend explicitly on the dimension $n$. The results hold for any fixed $n$ as $\varepsilon\to0$.
- Assumption 1.7 (unique minimal saddles, a gap $\delta$ between the two lowest barriers) is needed for r2 and r3. The paper notes that the generic case of two minima with different energies has a log-Sobolev constant different from the Poincaré constant by a factor of order $1/\varepsilon$.
