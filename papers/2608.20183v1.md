summary: "The paper gives the first deterministic algorithm that computes the local real log canonical threshold (RLCT), the learning coefficient that sets the $\\lambda\\log n$ term of the marginal likelihood of a singular model, for any polynomial in two variables over the rationals. It characterises when Varchenko's Newton-polygon method fails to terminate, and bounds the number of steps by $(d_y+\\tfrac12)d_x$. The algorithm applies to two-parameter models whose Kullback-Leibler distance is contact equivalent to a polynomial. For polynomial neural networks with repeated weights it gives exact rational learning coefficients up to depth 5, which decrease with depth and activation degree, and it is competitive with SGLD estimates."
