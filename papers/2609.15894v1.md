summary: "The paper introduces Quenched Ensemble Sampling (QES), which keeps the monotonically decreasing energy path of nested sampling but replaces the hard constraint by a soft microcanonical factor $(E-U)_+^\\nu$, so gradient-based kernels such as MALA can be used in high dimension. An exact augmentation shows that the marginal over $x$ is the posterior. On a spike-slab model with a first-order transition, QES estimates the marginal likelihood correctly while tempered SMC is about 2.3 nats low. On small tanh networks QES matches NUTS and MCLMC in predictive accuracy and supports model comparison between activations. On a lattice $\\phi^6$ theory it crosses a first-order transition and estimates the partition function."
