summary: "The paper bounds the generalisation gap of data-dependent Markov processes whose stationary law is Gibbs with respect to a data-independent base measure. A generalised second law of thermodynamics keeps the divergence of the law at time $t$ from the initial law bounded by the expected potential at initialisation, and a PAC-Bayes argument turns this into a gap bound. For continuous Langevin dynamics with weight decay or a bounded domain, the bound is $\\sqrt{(\\beta\\,\\mathbb{E}L(\\theta_0)+\\ln(1/\\delta))/N}$ and holds at every time, without mixing, dimension or smoothness assumptions. It is non-vacuous when $\\beta=O(N)$, and SGLD runs on MNIST, Fashion-MNIST and SVHN illustrate this."

- Read the HTML main text and the numerical-experiment appendix; appendix proofs and the later tables were not checked line by line.
- Filed as `gd` because the bound is for Langevin and other Markov processes as dynamics, valid at any time without reaching equilibrium. The temperature dial is therefore `not-applicable` under the GD-only rule, although $\beta$ is the central quantity.
- The bound needs $\ell_2$ regularisation or a bounded domain matching the initialisation, as the paper states.
- op-02 is touched because the bound needs no mixing assumption, but it concerns the generalisation gap and not the posterior mass of large-error functions.
- The Table 2-4 anchor points to the start of the experiments section A6.SS1.
