summary: "The paper argues that Bayesian classification lacks a way to state beliefs about aleatoric uncertainty, and that this explains much of the cold posterior effect. The softmax likelihood equals a Dirichlet observation model, in which a posterior temperature $T$ adds $1/T$ pseudo-counts to the observed class. The limiting law of SGLD with data augmentation is shown to be a tempered posterior on the augmented data with temperature $K>1$, so augmentation softens the likelihood and makes cold posteriors natural. A noisy Dirichlet model with a controlled noise parameter matches or beats tempering on CIFAR-10 and Tiny ImageNet, with optimal settings that track label noise and augmentation strength."
