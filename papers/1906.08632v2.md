summary: "The paper proves that online SGD in a teacher-student two-layer network with Gaussian inputs, large input dimension and finitely many hidden units is described by closed ODEs for overlaps between student and teacher weights. It uses them to compute the final generalisation error of over-parameterised students with $K\\ge M$ units. Training only the first layer, the error grows linearly in $K-M$ for sigmoidal, linear and ReLU students. Training both layers, sigmoidal students instead average several units per teacher unit, and the error falls as $1/K$, while linear and ReLU students show a constant error."

- Thm 1.1 is stated for sigmoidal students (bounded $g$ with bounded derivatives up to order two), hence the erf override on r1; ReLU and linear cases are treated by experiment only.

- Weight decay, mini-batches, MNIST inputs and finite training sets were tested only in the Supplementary Material, and are summarised in the main text (Sec. 2, Discussion).
