- Read the abstract, Secs. 1 to 6 and the figure captions; the appendices (A to I: harmonic expansions, proof of Prop 1, replica calculation, extra numerics) were skimmed only. The arXiv record has no journal reference, hence `preprint`.
- The exponents in Eq. (3.4) are not proved. The paper derives them from an ansatz for the predictor's spherical-harmonic coefficients, checked by an explicit calculation for $d=2$ and the replica method for the lazy case (App. F), and says there is no general rigorous framework; hence `phys`.
- Feature regime = the mean-field minimum-norm solution of Eq. (2.5), or in practice a wide network with L1 or weight-decay regularisation, or small initialisation. The paper observes that gradient descent needs very long to reach the minimum-norm solution for $d=2$ (Fig. 4 caption).
- r4 compares two training regimes of gradient descent (lazy against feature), not Bayes against GD, so `compared_other_rule` is `no`.
- No open problem is listed: op-03 and op-10 concern posteriors, and this paper has no Bayesian part.
