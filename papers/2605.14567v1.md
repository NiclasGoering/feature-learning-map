- Read the full HTML main text (Secs. 1–5); the appendices (spectral estimator, resolvent expansion in App. C, Wiener-chaos computations in App. E) were not read line by line. The statements follow the main-text theorems.
- The learner is a layer-wise spectral algorithm (top eigenvectors of an empirical degree-$q$ Hermite moment matrix, then a low-dimensional ridge fit), neither Bayes nor GD. `learning_rule: [gd]` is the closest bucket; Remark 2.1 links the estimator to GD only through a cited paper (Tabanelli et al. 2026), so the GD relation is not checked here. Architecture is `other` because the learner is not a standard network; the target is a three-stage hierarchical polynomial model.
- `staircase-of-directions` (T6) is used because features are recovered in order of strength as $n$ grows; strictly, the transitions come from a spectral procedure rather than training dynamics, so the type is the closest, not an exact fit.
- Thm 3.1 requires small $\varepsilon$ ($\varepsilon<q/2$, and $\varepsilon<q/(1-2\gamma)$ for nonlinear $g$), and the nonlinear readout is covered only for $0<\gamma<1/2$ (Assumption 3.1). The claim that shallow orthogonally invariant kernels need $n=\omega(d^{2q})$ is cited from Mei et al. 2022, not proved here.
- The paper says its rates match Defilippis et al. (2026a) with $d$ replaced by $D\sim d^q$; those papers have no confirmed arXiv ID here, so no relation is recorded. op-06 is touched only as a gradient-free analytic result on hierarchical data; there is no Bayesian analogue.
