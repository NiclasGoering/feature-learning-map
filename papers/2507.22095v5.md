summary: "The paper studies deep networks whose weights are Gaussian given random node variances, which makes weights dependent and possibly heavy-tailed, and which have a Gaussian-mixture prior limit at large width. It gives conditions under which the order of the sequential width limits does not matter. For a Gaussian likelihood it identifies the infinite-width posterior as a Gaussian mixture under a tilted measure, whose coordinates are dependent and not identically distributed, provided the random limiting covariance is almost surely invertible. Sufficient conditions on activation, Lévy measures and inputs ensure this. Monte Carlo simulations of two models illustrate the convergence."

- Read Secs. 1-7 (statements and the numerics); the proofs and Appendix A were not checked.
- The model is that of [26] in the paper (Gaussian weights with random node variances); the paper is about a sequential limit with fixed order, which Theorem 2 relaxes under conditions.
- New mechanism `posterior-kernel-reweighting` is introduced for r2 and r4 (Theorem 3, Remark 2); T3 is the closest type, although the reweighted kernel stays random rather than deterministic. See the PR description.
- Grade C for r2 since the limit law needs sampling the random kernel under the prior and reweighting (the paper's own Monte Carlo estimator, Sec. 7.3).
- The paper mentions 2502.04247 (Student-t limit) as another non-fixed-variance posterior result; no relation is recorded.
