one_line: "Exact gradient-flow solutions show how the conserved layer imbalance sets lazy versus rich learning; upstream initialisation speeds rich learning in ReLU networks."
summary: "The paper solves gradient flow exactly for a two-layer linear network with one hidden neuron and layer-specific learning rates. The quantity $\\delta=\\eta_w a^2-\\eta_a\\|w\\|^2$ is conserved and sets the geometry of the trajectory: balanced ($\\delta=0$), upstream ($\\delta>0$) or downstream ($\\delta<0$). The analysis extends to wide and deep linear networks and, approximately, to two-layer piecewise linear networks. In linear networks rapid rich learning needs balance; in nonlinear networks upstream initialisation gives rapid rich learning through changing activation patterns. Experiments on MNIST, CIFAR-10, a hierarchical model and a grokking task support this."
