summary: "The paper builds an effective field theory of multilayer perceptrons at initialisation: Gaussian weights and biases are integrated out and ghost fields are added to handle the determinant. A Feynman rule gives depth recursions for the kernel and for the connected four-, six- and eight-point functions of preactivations, each at leading order in $1/n$, and for the two-point function at next-to-leading order. The same diagrams show that one condition on the linearised flow, Eq. (31), turns exponential into power-law scaling for all connected correlators near the fixed point; it holds for the standard critical tuning of scale-invariant and $\\mathcal K^\\star=0$ activations. Ensembles of width-300, depth-30 ReLU and tanh networks confirm the power laws for two- and four-point functions, and training is not treated."

- RG record: sense (b), an effective theory of finite-width MLPs at initialisation in which the layer index is the RG scale (layer 1 is the "ultraviolet" boundary, text after Eq. (11)); the paper calls the depth recursions "RG flow", an analogy rather than a Wilsonian flow over scales. Integrated out: weights and biases (Eq. (2)), then all neurons and ghost fields except those of layer $\ell$, giving a tower of EFT actions with couplings $\mu^{(\ell)},\lambda^{(\ell)}=O(1/n)$ (Eqs. (16), (17)). What flows: the kernel $\mathcal K_0$ (Eq. (11)), $\mathcal K_1$ at NLO (Eq. (A.22)) and the connected $V_4,V_6,V_8$ at leading order (Eqs. (14)+(15), (A.29), Table 1), with linearised flow $\chi$ (Eqs. (24), (27), (30)) and one criticality condition, Eq. (31); each correlator is taken at leading order in $1/n$ and the two-point function at first order, with depth $L\ll n$ and no $L/n$ resummation. Maturity: diagrammatic derivation without proofs, checked numerically at $n=300$, $L=30$ for two- and four-point functions only (Figs. 2-5); nothing beyond first order, no training.

- Scope of the criticality claim (r1, r2): the paper says that tuning implies Eq. (31) "at least for" the scale-invariant and $\mathcal K^\star=0$ classes, at leading order in $1/n$. The argument concerns the linearised response of $V_{2k}^{(\ell)}$ to $V_{2k}^{(\ell-1)}$ (Eqs. (27), (30)); the paper does not derive the exponents $\gamma,\gamma_k$. Sec. I.4 notes that $O(1/n)$ corrections naively give $e^{\pm\ell/n}$, harmless for $L\ll n$, and may shift exponents (citing Roberts et al.); the claim that $V_{2k}$ is $O(1)$ is cited to Hanin, not derived. The numerics test two- and four-point functions only.
