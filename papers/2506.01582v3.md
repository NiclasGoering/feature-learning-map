summary: "The paper introduces the attention-indexed model, in which labels depend on bilinear token interactions through learnable $d\\times d$ matrices of extensive rank. For Gaussian inputs, $n/d^2\\to\\alpha$ and finite sequence length and depth, a replica computation gives the Bayes-optimal estimation and generalisation errors and an AMP algorithm with a state evolution. For one softmax layer the error reduces to the linear single-token quadratic case with a rescaled sample ratio, with an explicit strong-recovery threshold. For a hardmax target with two tokens, the error decays as a power law with no finite threshold. Averaged Adam reaches the Bayes-optimal error numerically, while single runs do not."

- Read the full main text (Secs. 1-4); the appendices (replica derivation, single-layer reductions, numerical details) were not read.
- The paper says Result 3.1 is derived with the replica method and is believed to be provable by adapting earlier rigorous work on quadratic networks; Results 4.1 and 4.3 are special cases. Status is therefore `phys`.
- Width: key/query width scales as $r=\rho d$ and $n\sim d^2$, so `n` is proportional to the parameter count; `high-dim` and R6 follow the convention used for Cui et al. 2023.
- Results for $L>1$ are stated but the paper has no worked $L>1$ example; the matrix denoising problem Eq. (22) is called open for non-diagonal $\Delta$. Depth is listed as both slugs for this reason, while worked results use one layer.
- r5: GD details (Adam, loss Eq. (33), averaging over initialisations) are in the main text; step size is not given there, hence `other`.
