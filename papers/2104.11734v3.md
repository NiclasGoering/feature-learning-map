summary: "The paper computes the exact marginal prior over the output preactivations of a single input for finite fully connected networks without biases and with iid Gaussian weights. For deep linear networks the density is a Meijer G-function depending on the hidden widths only through differences from the output width. A ReLU network's prior is a binomial mixture of linear-network priors of smaller widths plus a point mass at zero. Exact moments reproduce the sub-Weibull tail parameter $d/2$. A multivariate Edgeworth expansion recovers the known $1/n$ corrections but is sub-Gaussian, so it misses the heavy tails."
