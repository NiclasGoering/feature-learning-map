summary: "This research monograph builds an effective theory of fully connected networks as a perturbative expansion in 1/n, where n is the width. Preactivations at initialisation are nearly Gaussian, with interactions between neurons that grow with the depth-to-width ratio L/n, which acts as the effective depth. At infinite width, both Bayesian inference and gradient descent give a fixed kernel and no representation learning. At finite width, interlayer interactions let the posterior shift the hidden representation, and the differential of the neural tangent kernel makes the kernel evolve under gradient descent. The authors state they checked their formulas numerically but do not print experiments."

- Read from the PDF; there is no HTML version (arxiv.org/html returns 404), so `anchor: none` and `links.html: not-available`. The book has about 450 pages. First pass: table of contents, preface, Chapter 0, Secs. 6.4.1-6.4.3, the introduction of Chapter 11 and Sec. ∞.2.3. Verification pass (2026-11-04): Secs. 3.3-3.4, 4.3-4.5, 5.4, all of Ch. 6, Sec. 8.3 (Eqs. 8.41-8.71), Sec. 10.2.4. r4 and r5 rest on chapter introductions and were not re-checked.
- The Bayesian results use the deterministic hypothesis (6.9), i.e. exact conditioning on the labels (noise-free, temperature to zero); output noise (6.10) is only mentioned as an alternative. Footnote 6 says noise equals shifting $C_b^{(L)}\to C_b^{(L)}+\sigma_\varepsilon^2$; that holds for a single input only (a bias is shared across samples, noise is not).
- Footnote 35 (Sec. 6.4.3) claims the posterior update of layers below the penultimate is $O(1/n^2)$. The book's own Eqs. (8.54) and (8.70) give $O(1/n)$: the interlayer transfer coefficient is the susceptibility $\partial K^{(L)}/\partial K^{(L-1)}$, which is of order one. For linear activations $\mathbb E[\hat G^{(L)}\mid z^{(L-2)}]=C_W^2O(z^{(L-2)})$ exactly. This agrees with 2106.00651 (Eq. 8). r3 therefore states the penultimate-layer result only.
- Paper-level fields are those of the Bayesian chapters (`likelihood_scaled: no`, GD step and time `not-applicable`); r4 and r5 override them with `learning_rule: [gd]`. Infinite-width GD equals exact Bayes only when only the last layer is trained, Eqs. (10.46) and (10.48).
- r3 uses `kernel-grows-along-target`; `posterior-kernel-reweighting` (pull request #5) may fit better once merged. Not recorded for lack of slots: the exact deep-linear prior, Eqs. (3.45)-(3.57) (a $\chi^2$-product scale mixture with a log-normal limit at fixed $L/n$), and the evidence preference for criticality, Eq. (6.48).
