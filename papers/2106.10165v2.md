summary: "This research monograph builds an effective theory of fully connected networks as a perturbative expansion in 1/n, where n is the width. Preactivations at initialisation are nearly Gaussian, with interactions between neurons that grow with the depth-to-width ratio L/n, which acts as the effective depth. At infinite width, both Bayesian inference and gradient descent give a fixed kernel and no representation learning. At finite width, interlayer interactions let the posterior shift the hidden representation, and the differential of the neural tangent kernel makes the kernel evolve under gradient descent. The authors state they checked their formulas numerically but do not print experiments."
