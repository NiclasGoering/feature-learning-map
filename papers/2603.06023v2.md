one_line: "Large deviation principle for the covariance tensors of Gaussian CNNs with many channels; the posterior obeys the same LDP as the prior, a lazy signature."
summary: "The paper considers multidimensional CNNs with Gaussian weights, written through a patch-extractor formalism that covers strides, padding and pooling, in the regime where all hidden channel numbers grow proportionally at fixed depth, spatial size and number of data points. The conditional covariance tensors concentrate on a deterministic recursion, and the output converges to a Gaussian process. The main theorem is a large deviation principle for these tensors, with a layer-by-layer rate function. With a Gaussian likelihood, the posterior law of the last covariance obeys the same principle as the prior, so the posterior is lazy. The fully connected case is recovered as a special case."
