- Read the full 1995 letter (PDF only, 12 pages including one figure); there is no HTML version, so `html: not-available` and all anchors are `none`. The letter has no numbered sections, so `loc` names the equation or the part of the text. The entropies of the volumes beyond the large-$K$ limit are not written out ("for lack of space"), and stability and one-step-RSB details are deferred to a paper in preparation.
- `venue` is `preprint`: the arXiv record has no journal reference and the first page does not state one.
- Regime R6 and `high-dim` are used because the setting is the classical Gardner/replica one with $P=\alpha N$ and many inputs. The statement "$\epsilon_g=1/2$ then $\Gamma/\alpha$" for parity is attributed by the authors to a Bayesian result of Opper (1994), reinterpreted here.
- The RS solution is checked for local stability against the 011 and 122 replicon modes: it is marginally stable as $K\to\infty$, so the authors expect it to be exact in that limit; for finite $K$ they report that RSB is needed (e.g. the RS $\alpha_R=5.4$ and 3.5 for $K=3$ overshoot the one-step values).
- The committee-machine formulae for $\epsilon_g$ were read from a PDF text layer with some garbled symbols; $2\Gamma/\alpha$ and the cross-over to $\Gamma/\alpha$ should be checked against the printed page.
- $\Gamma\simeq0.62$ is defined in the paper through an integral involving $H(u)\ln H(u)$.
