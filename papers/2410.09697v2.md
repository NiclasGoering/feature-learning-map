one_line: "Geometric tempering of Langevin dynamics converges in KL under log-Sobolev bounds, but can worsen functional inequalities and take exponential time, even for unimodal targets."
summary: "The paper analyses Langevin dynamics run along a geometric path between an easy proposal and a hard target. Upper bounds in KL divergence, in continuous and discrete time, depend on the log-Sobolev constants of the intermediate laws. For strongly log-concave pairs the optimal schedule is closed form, and plain Langevin is optimal when the target is at least half as peaked as the proposal. Lower bounds show that the path can have exponentially worse functional inequalities than both ends. On a bimodal example, and on a unimodal target with good inequalities, total-variation error stays bounded below unless the run time is exponential."
