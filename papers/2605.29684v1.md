one_line: "An Equivalent Wishart Ansatz reduces deep Bayesian MLPs at $P\\propto N$ to $L$ scalar order parameters renormalising the NNGP kernel, tested up to depth about 10."
summary: "The paper proposes an Equivalent Wishart Ansatz for the hierarchical empirical kernels of fully connected nonlinear networks of fixed depth $L$ in the proportional regime. A large deviation analysis then gives an effective action in $L$ scalar order parameters whose product rescales the NNGP kernel. Extensions cover multiple outputs and convolutional layers, where a hierarchical local renormalisation appears. Posterior sampling on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and synthetic data agrees well with the theory up to depth $O(10)$, with two systematic deviations at large depth and load, one of them an abrupt metastable drop in the error. In the mean-field parametrisation the theory still predicts the error, and the gain over standard scaling comes from lower predictor variance."

- Read the abstract, introduction, Secs. II–IV, VI.3–VI.6 and VII in the HTML of v1; Sec. V.1 and the appendices were not read in detail. `venue` is `preprint` (no journal reference).
- The ansatz is approximate. The paper presents the large deviation principle for the $q_\ell$ as numerically supported (rate-function checks, Figs. 2 and 3) and not proved, so everything is `phys`. The paper says finite-size effects accumulate with depth and the error of the ansatz grows slowly with $L$.
- Result r4: the transient-phase picture makes the sampling itself hard, so `mixing: critical` was used, although the theory does not itself predict coexisting phases. The paper says the abrupt drop is not a sampler artefact (several samplers, sharper with larger $N$, $P$), but no mechanism is known. A checker may want to set mixing to `high`.
- Result r5 uses $\mu$P only through a rescaled readout prior and $T\propto1/N$ at finite sizes ($N\sim10^3$); the paper notes that SP and $\mu$P cannot be compared at the same finite temperature. It also says that at larger $N$ the ansatz could fail to capture a bias difference. `op-07` and `op-08` are listed because the ansatz is a $P\propto N$ $\mu$P-regime baseline and it misses the class-block kernel structure.
- The arXiv ID 2605.29684 is cited by the repository's reference note as "Baglioni et al. 2026".
