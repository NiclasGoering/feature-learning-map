summary: "For a student MLP with the teacher's architecture, hidden widths $k_l=\\gamma_l d$ and $n=\\alpha d^2$ Gaussian samples, the paper derives replica-symmetric free entropies for one hidden layer with generic activation, two hidden layers and deeper networks with restricted Hermite content. Below a threshold $\\alpha_{\\rm sp}$ the network sits in a universal phase that matches a quadratic approximation of the target. Beyond it, hidden units specialise to the teacher's, first those with large readouts, and layer by layer in deep networks. HMC, ADAM and an extended GAMP-RIE test the theory, and specialisation is algorithmically hard for some targets."
