summary: "The paper analyses online spherical SGD on Gaussian single-index data for an update oracle $\\psi_\\eta$ that mixes a correlational term with a term scaled by a learning rate $\\eta$. Theorem 3.3 gives the number of iterations for weak recovery as a minimum over Hermite coefficients $\\mu_i(\\eta)$, which produces phase transitions in $\\eta$. For batch-reuse SGD the complexity moves from $d^{(p-1)\\vee1}$ to $\\tilde\\Theta(d)$ as $\\eta$ crosses powers of $d$. A new alternating-SGD variant, with a readout step before the first-layer step on the same sample, shows the same transition."

- Read Sections 1 to 6 of the HTML version in full; the appendices (proofs, C.4 on deeper networks, D on experiments) were not read.
- Venue taken from the arXiv comment "NeurIPS 2025".
- The framework is for online algorithms with polynomial update oracle $\psi_\eta$ and spherical weights; only weak recovery is analysed. The paper takes strong recovery and the ridge fit of the readout, at $\tilde\Theta(d)$ samples, from earlier work.
- r4 (deeper networks) is stated in the main text with its proof and assumptions in App. C.4, where it is checked only for a three-layer network with $\sigma(z)=z^2$.
- `hidden-progress` is reused from the label introduced in this pull request by Dandi et al. (2402.03220) for the same-sample effect. For the alternating-SGD variant the stored label comes from the readout step rather than from a first-layer step, which I judged to be the same effect.
