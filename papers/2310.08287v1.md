summary: "The paper studies the distribution of weights of deep networks on image tasks; its posterior is by definition the law of SGD solutions from random initialisation, estimated by 1000 independently trained checkpoints. It writes this law as a continuous mixture of discrete mixtures over scaling and permutation symmetries, and poses a convex min-mass problem showing that weight decay does not remove scaling symmetry. Approximate Bayesian methods and SGHMC are compared to that SGD-ensemble reference by maximum mean discrepancy, with and without symmetry removal. Multi-mode methods are closest (deep ensembles trivially, being the reference method) and give better out-of-distribution detection. Independently trained ResNets show no functional collapse, and weights rarely permute late in training."

- Read the main text (Secs. 1-8), Table 1, App. B (training recipes, Table 2) and the proofs of Props. 3, 4 and 6 (App. C.4-C.6); App. D not checked. The arXiv listing gives no venue.
- The "Bayesian posterior" here is the law of independently SGD-trained networks (1000 checkpoints), not an exact posterior. Hence `learning_rule: [bayes, gd]`, with GD-only overrides for r2 and r4. Prop. 1 is stated for networks trained with SGD; its proof (Eqs. 84-88) uses only that the law is permutation-invariant because the initialisation is layer-wise iid (Props. 3-4), so it holds for SGD solutions and for a Gibbs posterior with an iid prior alike; r1 now carries both rules.
- Regime tag R6 is a judgement call: no regime fits a non-asymptotic posterior. `high-dim` is put on r3 only (ResNet-18 on CIFAR-100 and TinyImageNet, parameters far exceed samples) to satisfy the tag rule.
- Table 1 shows exceptions: Laplace has lower MMD than SWAG in some single-mode rows, and the Bayes-by-backprop BNN has no entry on TinyImageNet. Calibration (ECE) does not follow posterior quality.
- Table 1 is reported in percent (permille for ResNet-18 MMDs); no seeds or error bars are given in the main text.
- r3 is not a test of GD against a posterior: the reference is the GD-ensemble law itself, so deep ensembles match it by construction (App. B.4, different checkpoints). The one stochastic-gradient MCMC sampler (SGHMC, single chain, public repo; hyper-parameters not given in App. B) is far from it. SGHMC has no sampler value in the vocabulary; it sits under `other`.
