one_line: "Bayesian posterior of networks with fixed shared gatings and learned linear units, solved at $P/N\\to\\alpha$ with a matrix-valued kernel renormalisation."
summary: "The paper introduces Globally Gated Deep Linear Networks, in which a fixed bank of $M$ nonlinear gating units is shared by all neurons and layers while the weights are linear and learned. For the Gibbs posterior at zero temperature in the limit $N,P\\to\\infty$ with $P/N$ fixed, backward integration gives an $M\\times M$ order parameter that reshapes the Gaussian-process kernel across gating branches. Unlike the scalar renormalisation of deep linear networks, the mean predictor and bias now change with width, regularisation and depth. The theory is compared with Langevin sampling and gradient descent on synthetic ReLU-teacher tasks and MNIST, and extended to multi-task learning."

- Read the full HTML of v2 (main text, Appendix A derivation and the start of Appendix B/C); a later check of the v2 pdftotext covered Secs. 2-6, App. A.1-A.2 and App. B.2, C.1-C.5. The published NeurIPS 2022 version was not compared with v2.
- Eq. (6) drops a factor $\sigma^2$: the stationarity of $H_1$ (Eq. 5) gives $U_1=\sigma^2[I-\ldots+\ldots]$, consistent with App. A ($U_1=\sigma^2(I+H_1)^{-1}$, Eq. 21) and with $U_1\to\sigma^2I$ in the GP limit; as printed it holds only for $\sigma=1$. At $M=1$ the corrected equation is the Li-Sompolinsky scalar equation at $L=1$. The readout is integrated at $T\to0$ before the thermodynamic limit (App. A, Eq. 3).
- `scaling.D`: the paper never states how $N_0$ scales; the numerics use $N_0=30$-$784$ at fixed values, with $P$ below the capacity $N_0\binom{M+L-1}{L}$ (Sec. 2). The Fig. 2(c-e) inputs are clustered Gaussians (App. C.2). Langevin checks (App. B.2, Eqs. 51-52) state no temperature.
- Label judgement: the paper's $\mathbf U_1$ reweights the kernel over $M$ gating branches, including off-diagonal terms. I reused `patch-scale-renormalised` because the FL type definition groups per-patch and per-branch scalars, but the matrix structure (cross-branch terms) goes beyond a diagonal per-branch scale. A checker may prefer a separate label.
- Architecture and activation are `other` because the model is a gated linear network with fixed Heaviside-type or k-means gatings, not in the vocabulary. `data_class: real-data-gram` is used as the closest class; the theory holds for any inputs through the Gram matrix $K_0$.
- Theory describes the Langevin equilibrium at $T\to0$; the main text compares it with plain GD averaged over initialisations, which the paper itself says it only matches qualitatively. Empirics scale and temperature were not extracted from Appendix C.
- Order parameters for $L$ layers have size $M^l\times M^l$, which limits the theory to small $M$ and $L$ (stated in Sec. 6).
