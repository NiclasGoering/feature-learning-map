summary: "For deep networks with Gaussian weights and biases and Lipschitz activations, the joint output on a finite input set is compared with the Gaussian process of the same infinite-width covariances in Wasserstein distance. The rate is the sum of inverse hidden widths when all infinite-width covariance matrices are invertible, and the sum of inverse square-root widths in general. A duality argument transfers these rates to the exact Bayesian posterior when the likelihood is bounded and Lipschitz in the training outputs, which includes the Gaussian likelihood. Constants are not tracked and no numerics are given."

- Read the full HTML of v1 (18 Dec 2023), including the statements of Theorems 4.1, 4.2 and Corollary 5.3. Proofs in Sec. 3 were skimmed, not checked.
- The constants $\mathsf c$ are implicit and depend on depth, input set, activation and likelihood, so the bounds give rates but no usable numbers. Grade is therefore `none`.
- Part (a) of Thm 1.1 needs invertibility of every $K^{(\ell)}$ on the input set, and for the posterior rate $1/n$ on train plus test inputs (Remark 5.4). The prefactor $n_\ell\gamma_p^{kn_\ell}$ depends on the output width and number of inputs.
- Weights are Gaussian with variance $1/n_{\ell-1}$ and unit-variance biases (Xavier-type). The paper's generalized architecture also covers other layer types, but only the fully connected case is stated in the introduction.
- op-02 is tagged because the posterior bound holds for the exact posterior without a sampler, but it is an asymptotic-in-width rate with unknown constants, not the two-sided certified bound the problem asks for.
