summary: "The paper studies tempered posteriors with Laplace approximations (KFAC and isotropic) of WideResNet22 and CNN models on CIFAR-10/100, SVHN and FashionMNIST. Test 0-1 loss is almost always best at the coldest temperature, and better calibration comes at the cost of accuracy. A PAC-Bayes argument (Alquier and Catoni bounds) shows that a temperature arises when bounding out-of-sample risk, and the Catoni bound tracks the measured test error. A proposition for a linearised Gaussian model shows the effective temperature depends on curvature, prior, data and the MAP estimate, so it cannot just fix a misspecified likelihood."
