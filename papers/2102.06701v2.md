one_line: "Four scaling regimes of test loss with dataset and model size: universal variance-limited exponent one, and resolution-limited exponents set by kernel spectrum and data-manifold dimension."
summary: "The paper classifies power-law scaling of the test loss with dataset size and parameter count into four regimes. In the variance-limited regimes, with large data or large width, smoothness of the loss gives corrections of order $1/D$ and $1/w$, independent of architecture and data. In the resolution-limited regimes the model resolves a smooth data manifold, which gives exponents of order $1/d$ in the manifold dimension. In linear random-feature teacher-student models all four regimes follow from the kernel spectrum, with a duality between the data and width exponents. Experiments on deep teacher-student models and on MNIST and CIFAR networks support the exponents."

- `learning_rule: [gd]` is a placement choice. The theory covers both trained networks (r1, r2, r4) and linear random-feature models fitted to a global optimum (r3); `inf-kernel` follows from the large-width equivalence to random-feature models.
