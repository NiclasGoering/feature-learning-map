one_line: "Bayes-optimal AMP state evolution sorts Gaussian multi-index directions into trivial, easy with threshold $\\alpha_c$, or hard, and describes the grand staircase."
summary: "For Gaussian multi-index models with $n=\\alpha d$ samples and a fixed number of indices, the paper studies weak recovery by Bayes-optimal approximate message passing, which is optimal among first-order iterative methods. A trivial subspace is learned in one iteration for any $\\alpha>0$. If it is empty, an easy subspace is learned from vanishing side information in $O(\\log(1/\\lambda))$ steps once $\\alpha$ exceeds a threshold $\\alpha_c$ given by a stability condition. Some directions, such as 3-sparse parities, have $\\alpha_c=\\infty$. Coupling to easier directions lets hard ones be learned in a grand staircase."

- Venue taken from the arXiv journal reference "AISTATS 2025".
- The learner is Bayes-optimal AMP, whose state evolution is rigorous for finitely many iterations (Gerbelot and Berthier). Statements about $O(\log d)$ steps from random initialisation (Conjecture 1) are not recorded as results; the paper supports them with simulations.
- r3: the numerical values for $z_1z_2$, $\mathrm{sign}(z_1z_2)$ and $p\ge3$ parity come from the closed forms of App. F, which I did not check.
- r4 pairs the FL type T5 with the label `staircase-of-directions`, which the vocabulary lists for T6, so the validator warns. I kept T5 because the staircase here is a sequence of fixed points of the Bayes-AMP state evolution, not a training dynamics.
