summary: "The paper studies the complexity of sampling the stationary distribution of a mean-field SDE, the minimiser of an entropy-regularised energy. It separates the error into propagation of chaos, proved at stationarity, and sampling of the finite-particle stationary law with off-the-shelf log-concave samplers. For pairwise interactions it obtains a sharp $\\tilde O(dk^2/N^2)$ chaos rate under weak interaction, and improved gradient-call complexities for several samplers. For general functionals, including mean-field two-layer networks with noisy gradient descent, it shows a log-Sobolev inequality uniform in $N$ whose constant is doubly exponential in the problem parameters."

- Read Secs. 1-4 of v4 from the HTML (introduction, assumptions, propagation of chaos statements, Table 1, Examples 10-13) and the statement of Cor. 25; the proofs in Apps. A-E were not read. The arXiv record gives no venue, so `preprint`.
- The paper is sampling and optimisation theory for McKean-Vlasov dynamics; two-layer networks appear only as the motivating application (Sec. 4.2). `learning_rule: bayes` follows the rule for stationary laws of mean-field Langevin dynamics. Regime tag R3 is taken for the mean-field limit at fixed data; r1 and r2 are about generic pairwise interactions, so their architecture is overridden to `other`.
- The complexity bounds in r2 and r4 are in the metric $\frac{\sqrt\alpha}{\sigma}W_2$ and $\sqrt{\mathsf{KL}}$ respectively, with $\kappa$ the condition number. Rates in the paper's Table 1 differ per assumption set; only two entries are quoted.
- r3's constant is the paper's own caveat (Sec. 4.2 "Limitations"): doubly exponential in $B^2/\lambda\sigma^2$ (singly exponential in $\beta d/\lambda$, Cor. 25); Chewi et al. (2409.10440) later improve it to a single exponential.
- Full-text check (v4 pdf, incl. App. A.3, B.3): r5 (Thm 5) added; it was missing and is the only chaos result of the paper that applies to two-layer networks (r1, r2 need a translation-invariant pairwise $W(x-y)$). Its proof gives $\mathsf{KL}(\mu^{1:N}\|\pi^{\otimes N})\le33\beta\bar C_{\rm LSI}d/\sigma^2$ via the entropy toast inequality (Lemma 19); the factor $d$ comes from the Poincaré bound on $\mathrm{var}_\pi$. r3-r5 typed T3 (stationary law of mean-field Langevin dynamics), not T0.
- Conventions: Eq. (2.3) is $\propto\exp(-\frac{2N}{\sigma^2}F_0-\frac\lambda{\sigma^2}\|x\|^2)$, with $F_0=\sum_i\ell$ summed over data; so $T=\sigma^2/2$ and the Gaussian prior $\mathcal N(0,\frac{\sigma^2}{2\lambda}I)$ has variance proportional to $T$. Assumptions 6 and 8 need bounded neurons with bounded derivatives (e.g. $\tanh(a)\tanh(\langle w,x\rangle)$, Sec. 4.2): a trainable linear readout $a\varphi(w\cdot x)$ is not covered.
