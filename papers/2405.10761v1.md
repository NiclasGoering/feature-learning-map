one_line: "Writes the deep Bayesian network prior as a mixture of Gaussian processes and derives forward-backward kernel equations whose adaptation to data is largest near criticality."
summary: "The paper shows that the prior over outputs of a deep fully connected network is exactly a superposition of Gaussian processes whose kernels fluctuate with variance of order $1/N$. A large-deviation argument gives forward-backward equations for the maximum a posteriori kernels in all layers, exact for $P=\\alpha N$. Solving them perturbatively in $1/N$ shows the error signal at the output being propagated back to align each layer's kernel with the target. Adaptation is strongest slightly below the edge of chaos, and it grows when the output weight variance is reduced. Theory is compared with Langevin-sampled networks on XOR and MNIST."
