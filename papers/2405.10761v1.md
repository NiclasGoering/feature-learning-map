one_line: "Writes the deep Bayesian prior as a mixture of Gaussian processes and derives forward-backward kernel equations; adaptation peaks at weight variance well below criticality."
summary: "The paper shows that the prior over outputs of a deep fully connected network is exactly a superposition of Gaussian processes whose kernels fluctuate with variance of order $1/N$. A large-deviation argument gives forward-backward equations for the maximum a posteriori kernels in all layers, stated to be exact for $P=\\alpha N$. Solving them perturbatively in $1/N$ shows the error signal at the output being propagated back to align each layer's kernel with the target. Adaptation is strongest slightly below the edge of chaos, and it grows when the output weight variance is reduced. Theory is compared with Langevin-sampled networks on XOR and MNIST."

- Read the main text of v1 in full from the HTML version and the Langevin sampling appendix (App. G); other appendices (A, B, C, D, E, H, I) were skimmed for the statements cited.
- The forward-backward equations are stated exact for $P=\alpha N$ (Gärtner–Ellis argument, heuristic), but all nonlinear numerics use the leading $1/N$ expansion at fixed small $P\le32$ ($\alpha\le0.12$); only Fig. 6 solves the full linear equations (one hidden layer, $\alpha\le0.3$). Hence both R1 and R2 tags and `closure: [gartner-ellis, one-loop]`; the first-order step is tree-level linear response. For linear networks the rate (88) lacks the Wishart degrees-of-freedom term, so the $P\propto N$ claim is not supported.
- The adaptation peak $g_f$ lies well below $g_{\rm crit}$ (Sec. 4.2); the paper says adaptation is strongest slightly away from criticality (Sec. 5).
- `sampler: ula`: App. G is full-batch gradient descent with Gaussian noise and weight decay, i.e. unadjusted Langevin.
- Mixing is `high`: Langevin SGD with small $\kappa$ (e.g. $10^{-3}$), warm-up and thinning but no autocorrelation or R-hat diagnostics.
- `moves_mean` is `unknown`: the paper reports kernel alignment (CKA), not test error.
- Equation anchors checked against the HTML ids (`S3.E15`, `S4.E22`).
