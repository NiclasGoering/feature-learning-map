- Read the main text of v1 (Secs. 1-5) in full from the HTML version; the appendices (multi-layer extension J, Adam-versus-posterior K, field-theory toy model L, coverage M, numerics N) were skimmed through headings and captions.
- The theory uses the equivalent-kernel approximation and $d_{\rm model}\to\infty$ first; the paper lists both as limitations. The setting is a supervised copy task, not next-token prediction.
- Mixing is `critical`: the theory predicts competing minima near the transitions and SGLD runs are warm-started from Adam minima, with larger fluctuations at the crossover. Runs of Adam that did not reach the global minimum were filtered out for the linear case, on loss only (App. N.1).
- r4 combines the Bayesian and gradient-descent runs, so `compared_other_rule` is "yes" and the learning rule is overridden to both; the paper states the agreement and derives it for large $\chi$ in App. K, which I did not read.
- Approximations behind the two-parameter action: the data-term log-determinant is $O(1/\chi)$ relative to the $\chi L$ terms and dropped (Sec. 3.3, App. F); for softmax the pushforward Jacobian is dropped as a logarithmic correction (App. G); the restriction to the ansatz (11) is symmetry-motivated and its coverage is checked numerically (App. M).
- Internal mismatch: Sec. 3.3 quotes $P^*_{\hat c_1}\approx23$ and $P^*_{\hat c_G}\approx322$, while Sec. 4 and Fig. 1 place the linear transition at $P\approx100$; $\bar g_O,\bar g_W$ are not listed in App. N, so this could not be resolved.
- In Eqs. (16)-(17) the square root covers only the $\bar g$-dependent factor (HTML and PDF agree); with $\bar\sigma^2$ fixed this gives the stated $V^2$ and $L^{3/2}V^{1/2}$ scalings.
- `label_noise: none`: the copy labels are exact; $\sigma^2$ is the Gaussian likelihood (regularisation) noise.
- New mechanism `attention-pattern-switch`: the paper identifies the competition between uniform and copy attention as the cause of the transition. The related label `mixed-phase` concerns a first-order transition in neuron weight laws, not in an attention pattern.
