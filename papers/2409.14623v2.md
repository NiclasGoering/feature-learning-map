one_line: "Exact gradient-flow solution and NTK for two-layer linear networks with $\\lambda$-balanced initialisation, from sigmoidal rich dynamics to exponential lazy ones."
summary: "The paper solves gradient flow exactly for a two-layer linear network with whitened inputs and weights whose layer imbalance $W_2^\\top W_2-W_1W_1^\\top$ equals $\\lambda I$. The solution covers the network function, the representational similarity of the hidden layer and the finite-width NTK. Singular values follow sigmoidal curves as $\\lambda\\to0$ and exponential curves as $|\\lambda|\\to\\infty$, with a semi-structured lazy regime at large $|\\lambda|$. Funnel and inverted-funnel shapes show a delayed rich phase. The solutions are applied to continual, reversal and transfer learning and to fine-tuning."
