- Read Secs. 1-4 of the HTML v1; Appendix proofs were not checked. The theory assumes a linear final layer, Gaussian prior and Gaussian likelihood with positive noise, and an empirical NNGP kernel that converges (true for FCNs, CNNs and attention networks per the authors).
- The paper uses the NTK parametrisation only to keep the prior $\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ at all widths and states that standard parametrisation gives the same function-space prior; hence `parametrisation: ntk` and `inf-kernel`.
- Sampler is underdamped Langevin Monte Carlo without Metropolis correction, tuned for above 98% mean acceptance; ESS and $\hat R$ are used, with a single chain of 3M steps per width and three chains for ResNets, and the authors note some chains may be far from equilibrium at $n\ge10$K. Mixing is set to `low`; the claims concern relative ESS, not equilibrium coverage.
- `moves_mean` is `no` for all results because the paper concerns the posterior itself and its sampler; the mean predictor is that of the NNGP posterior.
