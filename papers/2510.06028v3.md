summary: "The paper gives data-dependent bounds on the true error of the Gibbs posterior in the interpolation regime, where random labels can also be fitted. The bound at low temperature involves a path integral of training errors at higher temperatures, so generalisation at low temperature is signalled by small training error at high temperature. For Markov processes that converge to the Gibbs posterior, such as Langevin Monte Carlo, the bounds hold along the whole trajectory and sharpen a result of Harel et al. 2025. Because rigorous constants are too large for deep networks, a heuristic calibration based on the training data alone gives close upper bounds on test error for networks trained with SGLD on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and SVHN, and a valid bound for random labels."
