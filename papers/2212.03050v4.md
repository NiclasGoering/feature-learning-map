one_line: "For convex mean-field Langevin dynamics under a uniform log-Sobolev inequality, the $N$-particle system tracks the mean-field law uniformly in time, with $O(1)$ entropic bias."
summary: "The paper studies mean-field Langevin dynamics for a convex functional of a measure, and the corresponding $N$-particle system, under a uniform log-Sobolev inequality. It proves $L^p$ convergence and hypercontractivity of the mean-field flow, an exponential rate for the particle system that is uniform in $N$ up to an $O(1)$ bias, and Wasserstein and entropic propagation of chaos that is uniform in time. The assumptions are checked for a regularised Coulomb system and for two-layer networks with weight decay and bounded features. A numerical experiment on a sine-cosine target shows the loss decaying exponentially at a rate independent of the number of neurons."

- Read the main text (Secs. 1-3) and the theorem statements; the proofs in Secs. 4-5 and the appendices were not checked.
- Placed as `[bayes, gd]` with R3 following the other mean-field Langevin entries (2202.01009, 2201.10469): the dynamics results are overridden to `gd`, and the stationary-law bias result r3 to `bayes`. The tag is a judgement call: the paper is about the particle approximation, with $P$ and the data law not varied.
- Thm 2.1 (exponential energy dissipation, improved to drop smoothness) and Prop 2.2-2.3 ($L^2$ convergence, hypercontractivity) are not separate results; the Coulomb example (Example 3) and Thm 3.1 (modified Bochner theorem for convexity of interaction functionals) are not covered by the five results.
- Cor 2.8 gives a rate $\kappa$ that the authors call far from the $O(1/N)$ of Thm 2.6 because finite-time coupling is used; they cite another work that reaches $O(1/N)$ in a weaker distance.
- Numerics (Sec. 3.3, Figs. 2-3): the loss decays exponentially at a rate independent of $N$ and the residual fits $\alpha/N+\beta$; the experiment uses a truncation without the bounded derivatives assumed in Example 4, and the discretisation error is ignored.
