summary: "The paper characterises the training dynamics of grokking on a one-layer transformer learning $(x^2+y)\\bmod 67$ with three observables: parameter mobility, replica correlation between perturbed copies of a run, and the fractal dimension of the weight trajectory. After memorisation, mobility collapses, replica correlation plateaus and ages with waiting time, and the fractal dimension falls to 1, which the authors read as glass-like kinetic arrest. A swap-Monte-Carlo-inspired plug-in, which swaps parameters within a layer when the fractal dimension drops, cuts the grokking time from about 3000 to 650 epochs. Large Gaussian noise and weight decay also accelerate generalisation, consistent with random exploration."
