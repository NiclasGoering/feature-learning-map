summary: "The paper studies Langevin Monte Carlo (LMC), SGLD, SGLD with control variates (SGLDFP) and SGD on a strongly log-concave posterior with $N$ data points and step size $\\gamma=\\eta/N$. It bounds Wasserstein distances between the four algorithms and the posterior. LMC and SGLDFP have invariant laws within $O(N^{-1/2})$ of the posterior, whereas for Bayesian linear regression the SGLD invariant law stays at distance $\\Omega(1)$. Expansions of the means and covariances show that SGLD and SGD are of order $\\Theta(\\eta)$ away from the posterior. Experiments on logistic regression, including Covertype, confirm these rates."
