- Read the abstract, Secs. 1-3 and the statement of Sec. 4 in the HTML v1; derivations (App. A-K) were read only for settings such as the HMC procedure. The paper says its derivations are non-rigorous and rely on posterior concentration and Gaussian cavity fields, hence `phys` and closure `gaussian`.
- Activation must have $\mu_2=0$ (odd activations); experiments use $\tanh(2x)$. Teacher weights are drawn from the Stiefel manifold. The Bayesian student is given the readout, which the paper says does not change the Bayes error at $k=\Theta(d)$. The paper calls the effect "feature learning" and not specialisation, but the mechanism (per-feature overlap turning positive) is the vocabulary's `specialisation`.
- HMC is started with the first $k_c$ rows at the ground truth and the rest random (App. K.2), and the authors note that this does not validate the prediction of $k_c$ itself, so mixing is `critical` and diagnostics `planted-vs-random`.
- Learning rate 0.003 and $10^4$ epochs for Adam (Fig. 1); features are re-normalised each epoch.
