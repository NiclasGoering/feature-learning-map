summary: "The paper studies the risk of kernel ridge regression with independent observations under a Gaussian universality assumption. It introduces the Signal Capture Threshold, which fixes the kernel principal components the predictor learns on average and the variance of the predictor along them. Finite-size bounds from a Stieltjes-transform analysis of generalised Wishart matrices give the expected risk and the expected training error. Their ratio is independent of the true function, so the risk can be estimated from the Gram matrix and noisy labels: the Kernel Alignment Risk Estimator. On MNIST and Higgs it matches the true risk and selects ridge and lengthscale like cross-validation."
