summary: "The paper tests three explanations of the cold posterior effect (CPE) in Bayesian neural networks: dataset curation, data augmentation and a bad prior. With ResNet-20 and SG-MCMC at six temperatures, full SVHN and CIFAR-10 without augmentation show no CPE. Synthetic curation, with trained networks as labellers, produces a CPE only when the test set is curated too. Random sub-sampling of a dataset without CPE creates one, which points to the prior, since its weight relative to the likelihood grows as data shrink. A toy problem with a well-specified one-hidden-layer network shows the same trend, and the authors conclude that the CPE has several causes."

- Read the main text and figure captions; the appendix experiments were not checked in detail. Only the toy size in Fig. 6 ($n=32$) was read.
- Regime tag R1 and `finite-proportional` are the closest schema group for a Bayes paper at finite width and data; the paper does not analyse proportional scaling. They are chosen only because Bayes entries need a regime tag.
- Mixing is entered as `high`: the conclusions rest on unadjusted cyclical SG-MCMC at temperatures down to $10^{-3}$ standing in for the tempered posterior; the only checks are Wenzel et al.'s diagnostics, and the paper itself says inaccurate inference cannot be excluded (Sec. 2).
- No preconditioning in the reported runs (App. D.1-D.2). Toy MLP: one hidden layer of 20 ReLU units, full batch; MNIST: three hidden layers of 20 units; IMDB: Wenzel et al.'s CNN-LSTM.
- Sec. 2 says the matching likelihood-tempered model has prior "variance scaled by $1/T$"; the algebra gives $T\sigma^2$ (r5 states the correct factor).
- r3: the CIFAR-10 sub-sampling run (App. E, Fig. 12) shows almost no effect, a qualification the main text does not draw. App. E.6 (CIFAR-10H) finds that over-weighting the likelihood removes the effect with fewer labellers than curation theory needs; not entered (five results).
- Relation targets 2008.05912 (Aitchison) and 2002.02405 (Wenzel et al.) are not yet in the map; the IDs were checked against the arXiv API.
