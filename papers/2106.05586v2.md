one_line: "Bayesian networks with data augmentation built into the likelihood, exact on finite orbits and tightly bounded otherwise, still show a cold posterior effect."
summary: "The paper builds data augmentation into the generative model of Bayesian neural networks by averaging either logits or class probabilities over the augmentation distribution, which gives a valid likelihood. On a finite orbit of augmentations the likelihood is exact, and on the full orbit multi-sample Jensen bounds give tight lower bounds that improve with the number of samples. Gradient-guided Monte Carlo on ResNet-20 and CIFAR-10 shows that the cold posterior effect persists in these principled models, so it is not an artefact of an invalid likelihood. Without augmentation the effect is minimal. The authors suggest that curation or a misspecified prior explains the rest."
