one_line: "Random-feature regression on power-law latent data gives test-loss power laws in samples and features, with a data-feature duality and a latent-size plateau."
summary: "The paper builds a joint model of a generative dataset, with a latent feature space of size $M$ and a power-law covariance spectrum, and a random feature map with $N$ features fitted on $T$ samples. A diagrammatic random-matrix calculation gives the test loss for any latent covariance, symmetric under exchange of $T$ and $N$. For a power-law spectrum the loss decays as $N^{-\\alpha}$ or $T^{-\\alpha}$ until the other resource bottlenecks it, and optimal regularisation removes the divergence at $N=T$. When $M$ is smaller than $N$ or $T$, scaling laws break down and the loss falls to zero or a noise floor."
