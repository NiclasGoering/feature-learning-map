summary: "The paper argues that global convergence guarantees for stochastic-gradient MCMC do not apply to degenerate neural-network loss landscapes, and that the practical target is local posterior sampling. It builds a benchmark on deep linear networks, whose local learning coefficient (LLC), the volume-scaling exponent at a minimum, is known exactly for special and generic optimal parameters. Five samplers (SGLD, RMSProp-SGLD, Adam-like SGLD, SGHMC, SGNHT) are compared by the relative error of their LLC estimates on models from 100K to 100M parameters. RMSProp- and Adam-preconditioned SGLD are least sensitive to step size and give the best mean-variance trade-off, so non-trivial local information is recovered even without global guarantees."

- Read in full (main text, App. A-G). r4 and r5 added in verification: the ground-truth theorems are cited (proofs in Aoyagi 2024, Lehalleur and Rimanyi 2024), and App. D.1 qualifies the r3 headline.
- Regime tag R1 and `finite-proportional` are wrong for this paper (fixed model, $n\\to\\infty$, no width limit) but the schema has no regime tag for finite-exact Bayes papers; kept only because Bayes entries need one.
- `dials.temperature: fixed`: $\\beta=\\beta_0/\\log n$ per sample goes to zero slowly (temperature grows) while $n\\beta\\to\\infty$; experiments use one fixed $\\beta$. The previous `to-zero` read the direction backwards.
- The learning-coefficient estimator is a posterior expectation at a tempered local posterior, so the paper is filed as `bayes`. Samplers run SGLD-type unadjusted dynamics, hence mixing `high`.
- The relation target 2308.12108 (Lau et al.) is taken from the paper's citation of Lau et al. 2025 and is not yet in the map; the ID was checked against the arXiv API.
- op-03 is touched because the paper states that global SGMCMC convergence is not guaranteed and tests local coverage only.
