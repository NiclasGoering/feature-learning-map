summary: "The paper explains why mean-field variational Bayesian neural networks collapse to the prior in overparametrised models. If the likelihood is invariant under translations or permutations, a mean-field posterior and an invariance-abiding mixture give the same predictive, but their ELBOs differ by a KL divergence, the invariance gap. In an overparametrised Bayesian linear model with translation invariance, the optimal mean-field fit reverts to the prior as the dimension grows, while the invariance-abiding optimum equals the true posterior. The analysis is then carried to neural networks, where each node has translation-invariant subspaces. No neural-network experiments are reported."
