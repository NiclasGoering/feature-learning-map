The Gärtner–Ellis theorem fails here because the moment generating function is not finite everywhere, so the proof uses exponential equivalence and the multidimensional Cramér theorem. The rate function is simplified to a minimisation over the last-layer kernel only. For ReLU the paper gives a power-series expansion of the log moment generating function, intended for numerical minimisation."

- Full text of v3 read (Secs. 1-3, incl. the proof in Sec. 2.2 and Prop. 2.6). Like its predecessor, this is a result about the prior (network at initialisation) with no likelihood. The Bayes label, `p_vs_n: fixed`, `closure: exact` and R0 are placeholders chosen to match how other prior-law results are tagged in the map.
- The LDP is for $Z/\sqrt{v(n)}$, i.e. outputs of mean-field size; at $z=\gamma_0y$ Eq. (1.9) equals the fixed-P kernel objective $\min\sum_\ell\gamma_\ell J_\ell+\tfrac{\gamma_0^2}2y^\top(K^L)^+y$ (zero label noise). The paper does not make this link.
- Sec. 3 assumes $q$ diagonal without justification ("As before, we can assume..."); for ReLU this is a restriction, not a change of basis. Eq. (3.1) also misses a factor $1/(2\sqrt\pi)$ (at $k=0$ the left side is $1/2$), so $R(\beta)$ and the first-order expansion (3.6) carry wrong constants. The proof of Prop. 2.6 has a typo ($gg^+g^+z=2g^+z$); the conclusion is unaffected.
- The Lemma 1.1 anchor `S1.Thmlemma1` points to the statement in the introduction; the proof is in Sec. 3.
- Venue: no journal reference on arXiv and none found by search, so `preprint` (another paper cites it as Statist. Probab. Lett., unverified).
