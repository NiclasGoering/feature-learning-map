summary: "The paper extends the large deviation principle for the output of deep Gaussian neural networks at initialisation from bounded continuous activations to activations that grow at most linearly, including ReLU, for any finite set of inputs. The Gärtner–Ellis theorem fails here because the moment generating function is not finite everywhere, so the proof uses exponential equivalence and the multidimensional Cramér theorem. The rate function is simplified to a minimisation over the last-layer kernel only. For ReLU the paper gives a power-series expansion of the log moment generating function, intended for numerical minimisation."
