one_line: "A dynamical mean field theory of two-stage gradient flow shows when rich pretraining helps, hurts or collapses fine-tuning in wide mean-field networks."
summary: "The paper studies pretraining on a source task followed by training on a target task in infinitely wide networks in mean-field/μP scaling, where both stages can learn features. For two-layer networks it gives a two-stage DMFT with history dependence on the source. In linear toy models, a data-rich source gives a rank-one spike along the source direction and fine-tuning always beats a random start. Finite source data adds a noise spike and crosstalk that can cause negative transfer, and ultra-rich pretraining collapses the kernel to rank one. Experiments on polynomial targets and CIFAR-10 agree with the theory."
