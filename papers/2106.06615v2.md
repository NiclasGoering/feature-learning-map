one_line: "Exact finite-width prior predictive density of Gaussian-weight ReLU networks via Meijer-G functions; depth fattens the tails, width restores Gaussianity, with a normal log-normal limit."
summary: "For a fully connected network with Gaussian weights and a single fixed input, the paper derives the exact density of the output at any width and depth, first for linear networks and then as a mixture of Meijer-G functions for ReLU, indexed by the number of active units per layer. All moments follow, and the kurtosis is $3((m+5)/m)^{L-1}$. When depth grows like a power of width, moments converge to Gaussian ones if depth grows slower than width and to a normal log-normal mixture if the two are proportional. The results are used to define generalised He priors with a specified output variance."

- Read the v2 HTML for Secs. 1–7; the appendix proofs (B for linear, C for ReLU) were not checked.
- The results hold for one fixed input of unit norm only. The paper states a stochastic-process extension and convolutional architectures as future work.
- The entry describes the prior (network at initialisation); `learning_rule: bayes` and R0 follow the convention used for other prior-law results. Thm 5.1 uses NTK parametrisation ($\sigma_1^2=1$, $\sigma_i^2=2/m$); the exact results use arbitrary layer variances.
- `relations`: Sec. 5.1 says it recovers Lee et al. 2018 and Matthews et al. 2018 (arXiv 1711.00165 is Lee et al.; I did not check the arXiv ID against the paper's bibliography beyond the title).
- The paper reports that Zavatone-Veth and Pehlevan concurrently derived similar finite-width prior predictive results (Sec. 2); I did not add a `same-result-as` relation because the paper says the two analyses differ in their limits.
- Sampler: the figures compare against $10^4$ forward samples of the prior, not a posterior sampler, so `sampler` is `none`.
