summary: "Earlier work showed that deep $L_2$-regularised networks minimise a rank of the learned function at large depth. This paper computes the next-order correction, a regularity measure $R^{(1)}$ that bounds the pseudo-determinant of the Jacobian and is subadditive under composition and addition. It trades a low inner dimension against irregularity of the feature maps, so the network can pick the right inner dimension. It proves a bottleneck structure: for depth large and norm close to minimal, almost all weight matrices are close to rank-$k$ maps and hidden representations have bounded norm. Experiments with depth 25 illustrate this."
