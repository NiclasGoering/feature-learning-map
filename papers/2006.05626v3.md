one_line: "Data fitting with a Radon-domain total-variation penalty has sparse solutions that are finite-width one-hidden-layer nets with truncated-power activations, trained with a path-norm-like penalty."
summary: "The paper poses a continuous-domain inverse problem: fit $N$ data points while penalising the total variation of a Radon-domain operator applied to the function. A representer theorem shows a minimiser exists that is a single-hidden-layer network with at most $N$ minus a null-space dimension units, for truncated power activations including ReLU, plus a polynomial term. The problem is shown equivalent to training a finite network with a weight-decay or path-norm regulariser. The underlying native space is a Banach but not a Hilbert space, unlike the kernel case. A Rademacher bound for the resulting networks is also given."
