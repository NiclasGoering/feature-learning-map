one_line: "Misclassification error of max-margin SVM and optimally regularised ridge classification decays as power laws set by capacity and source exponents; SVM is faster."
summary: "The paper studies kernel classification with Gaussian design, a power-law feature spectrum (capacity $\\alpha$) and power-law teacher coefficients (source $r$). Starting from the self-consistent equations of the Gaussian covariate model, applied in the $n\\ll p=\\infty$ limit, it derives the decay of the misclassification error with sample size for margin-maximising SVMs and for ridge classifiers. The SVM rate is $n^{-\\alpha\\min(r,1/2)/(1+\\alpha\\min(r,1/2))}$, faster than the optimally regularised ridge rate, and the worst-case SVM bounds are loose on this data class. Learning curves on MNIST, Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10 with RBF and polynomial kernels follow the predicted rates."
