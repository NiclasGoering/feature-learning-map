summary: "Starting from the dynamical mean-field action of infinite-width networks, the paper expands around the infinite-width solution to obtain the $O(1/\\sqrt N)$ fluctuations of kernels and predictions over initialisations. In the lazy limit the error covariance obeys a closed ODE. In the rich regime of two-layer and deep linear networks the kernel and error fluctuations are coupled, and feature learning improves the kernel signal-to-noise ratio. Large-step dynamics near the edge of stability stay close to the infinite-width equations. Experiments with tanh, linear and CNN models, including CIFAR-10, show 1/N corrections that break down when $P$ approaches $N$."

- Prop. 1, 3 and 4 are derived from a saddle-point (cumulant) expansion of the DMFT action, so they are marked `phys` even though they are numbered propositions. Grade B because the infinite-width kernel dynamics must be solved first (the propagator needs Monte Carlo in general).
- Left out because of the five-result limit: Sec. 6.2 (offline training with many samples or online learning at large $D$; leading corrections break down when $P\sim N$ or $D\sim N$, Fig. 4) and Sec. 8 (edge of stability: infinite-width discrete-time equations, Eq. (10), capture the dynamics and the predicted finite-size variance decreases after the kernel reaches $2/\eta$, Fig. 6).
- Fig. 7 uses a depth-6 ReLU CNN trained on CIFAR-10 with ensemble averaging; `finite-exact` is used for r5 because no asymptotic scaling is claimed there.
- The parametrisation is the mean-field / μP one with a feature-learning strength $\gamma$ ($\gamma\to0$ gives the lazy limit); r2 is overridden to `inf-kernel` for the lazy limit.
