summary: "A one-hidden-layer network is lifted to a convex energy $F$ on probability measures over neuron parameters, and an entropy term with weight $\\sigma^2/2$ makes the free energy strictly convex. The paper proves that its unique minimiser is characterised by a first-order condition involving the functional derivative of $F$. The marginal laws of the mean-field Langevin dynamics, the limit of noisy regularised gradient descent, converge in Wasserstein-2 to that minimiser for general smooth losses and activations, without homogeneity assumptions. It also shows a $2L/N$ gap between $N$ particles and the infinite-width optimum, and that the free energy decreases along the flow."

- Read the full main text (Secs. 1-3) of the HTML; the proofs in Secs. 4-6 and App. A were not read line by line. Anchors checked against the HTML. The venue is not stated on the first page of v3, so `preprint`.
- The paper's model is the one-hidden-layer network $\frac1N\sum_i c_i\varphi(\alpha_i\cdot z)$ with data law $\nu$ of compact support, hence `depth: [one-hidden-layer]` and `P: fixed`. Section 3 sets up a general $L$-layer network, but Cor 3.3 and the particle system are for one hidden layer. A truncation $\ell$ of the output weight is used.
- Learning rule: Prop 2.5 and 2.3 concern the stationary Gibbs-type minimiser (`bayes` per INGEST.md), while Thms 2.9 and 2.11 concern the Langevin dynamics. Thm 2.11 links the two and is marked as a comparison; no numerical comparison is made. Thm 2.4 is a statement about the minimum value of a loss and is marked `gd` with `finite-exact` width.
- The Gibbs reference measure $g=e^{-U}$ (typically Gaussian) plays the role of a prior: `prior_family: other`. Regime tag R3 is used because the stationary law is the mean-field minimiser at fixed data, as in the map's description of MFLD; the paper does not use the word Bayesian.
- The reference [39] in the text is named "Mei, Misiakiewicz and Montanari" but the reference list gives Mei, Montanari and Nguyen (1804.06561); the relation uses the latter.
- Exponential convergence is only mentioned in Remark 2.12 under extra conditions, citing Eberle, Guillin and Zimmer; it is not proved here.
- No feature-learning mechanism is analysed, so every result is `not-analysed`; T3 follows the map's note that mean-field Langevin minimisers are T3.
