summary: "The paper tests the volume hypothesis, which says that zero-training-error weights with good generalisation occupy most of weight space. It estimates the joint density of training and test accuracy for binary-weight convolutional networks with 30, 300 and 600 MNIST or Fashion-MNIST training images, using replica-exchange Wang-Landau sampling. The most likely test accuracy under random sampling is compared with gradient learning by BinaryConnect. The advantage of gradient learning generally diminishes as the data size grows, which the authors present as reconciling earlier small-sample and large-sample experiments. The density of test accuracy also sharpens with data size."

- Read the abstract, introduction, related work, method, experiments, results and the start of the Discussion; the discussion's remainder, the appendices and the figure content were not read, so Fig. 1 to 4 are cited by caption and text only.
- Regime: binary-weight networks with finite size. R6 with `high-dim` is a judgement call (nearest "classical thermodynamic" Bayes regime: density of states over train and test accuracy); the only width slug that is literally true is `finite-exact`. Number of weights grows with width, but the sample size is far below the weight count ($P/\text{params}<0.01$).
- Bayes side: a uniform prior over binary weights with zero training error, probed by the density of states (replica-exchange Wang-Landau, with six training accuracies and test-accuracy bins). `mixing: low` because the sampler is Metropolis-corrected with a flat-histogram criterion; no equilibrium diagnostic beyond convergence of the modification factor and run-to-run variability (Fig. 2) is described.
- Table 2 gives the numbers behind r1; the gap is not monotone in every row (for example Fashion-MNIST wide network under GD), so "generally" is the paper's own wording.
- The relation points to arXiv 2503.13145 (Yang et al., the "high-entropy advantage" paper), which is in the ingest queue; the paper names it as the large-data experiment it reconciles.
