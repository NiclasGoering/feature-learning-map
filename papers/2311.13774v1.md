summary: "The paper studies a three-layer residual network trained by layerwise gradient descent on Gaussian data, with a target $h=g\\circ p$ where $p$ is a degree-$k$ polynomial and $g$ a degree-$q$ polynomial. In stage 1 the inner two-layer map is fitted by a convex problem to the best degree-$k$ polynomial of $h$. An approximate Stein's lemma shows this polynomial is a multiple of $p$ for a class of spread-out features. Stage 2 fits the one-dimensional link by a random-feature model, so the total sample cost $\\tilde O(d^k)$ equals that of learning $p$ alone and is optimal for quadratic $p$; simulations with joint Adam training and $d\\le40$ agree."

- Read the PDF (v1 only; there is no later version). The arXiv HTML for v1 contained passages from an unrelated hierarchical-target paper mixed into the text in my fetch, so I took statements from the PDF and used the HTML only for element ids (Thmtheorem1, Thmcorollary1, Thmlemma2, S5.F2), which sit in the correct order in the HTML. Please recheck anchors.
- Training is layerwise with sample splitting: $u$ is trained for $T_1$ steps with weight decay, then $c$; $a,b,s,V$ stay at initialisation. `learned_layers: [other]` records this. Stage 1 is a convex kernel regression and stage 2 a random-feature fit, so the whole algorithm is not a kernel method but each stage is. Depth is `deep` (two hidden layers).
- The theorem needs Assumption 4 (spread-out feature, $L=\Theta(d)$ orthogonal blocks; orthogonally decomposable tensors and sums of sparse parities), $\sigma_1$ a degree-$k$ polynomial and $\sigma_2$ ReLU. Rates are $\tilde O$ with unspecified constants, hence grade none. The $\tilde\Omega(d^{kq})$ kernel lower bound is cited (Ghorbani et al.), not proved. Sample size, width and step counts are polynomial in $d$, so `p_vs_n` is `other`.
- Mechanism: I added `approximate-stein-extraction` because the paper itself identifies it (Sec. 4.3, Lemma 2) and no listed label describes an inner layer converging to a hidden polynomial feature through a convex low-degree fit. Type T6 since the change comes from the training stage, not a posterior. Lemma 2 is about the target only, so its GD step and time fields are overridden to not-stated.
- Venue is `preprint`: the first page and arXiv metadata give no venue. Parametrisation of the theory is not stated (only the experiments name μP).
