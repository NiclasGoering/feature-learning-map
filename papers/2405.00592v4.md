one_line: "A free-probability S-transform derivation of ridge regression, kernel and linear random-feature learning curves as a renormalised ridge, with scaling-law regimes and a variance-dominated regime."
summary: "The paper gives a self-contained introduction to the R and S-transforms of free probability and uses them to derive the generalisation error of linear ridge regression, kernel regression and deep linear random-feature models. In each case the explicit ridge is multiplicatively renormalised, once per source of randomness, by an S-transform factor. Under power-law spectra and targets the formulas yield the scaling laws in sample size and width, with all crossovers. They also show a variance-dominated regime in the overparameterised random-feature model, where initialisation variance hurts before the interpolation threshold. Nonlinear random features enter through additive feature noise."
