summary: "The paper classifies how $L$-hidden-layer MLPs trained by SGD behave at infinite width under abc-parametrizations, a family containing the standard, NTK and mean-field parametrizations. For tanh or σ-gelu it proves a dynamical dichotomy: a stable parametrization either learns features ($r=0$) or reduces to kernel gradient descent with fixed features ($r>0$), never both. The standard parametrization is stable only with learning rate $O(1/n)$ and is then in the kernel regime, while the Maximal Update Parametrization (μP) is the unique stable choice that updates every layer maximally. Limits are derived with the Tensor Programs Master Theorem, and exact μP limits of one-hidden-layer linear networks beat GP/NTK limits and finite networks on Word2Vec and Omniglot few-shot learning."

- Title is the arXiv title; the ICML version is titled "Tensor Programs IV: Feature Learning in Infinite-Width Neural Networks".
- `gelu` stands for the paper's σ-gelu (Def. H.1), a smoothed relu, not the standard GELU. The classification results (Thm 3.3 to Cor. 3.10, Thm 4.1, Thm 5.6) assume tanh or σ-gelu with small σ (Assumption 3.1); the limit derivation (Thm 7.4) only needs $\phi'$ pseudo-Lipschitz. The paper notes the dichotomy fails for linear $\phi$.
- Training time is $O(1)$ in width and the training sequence is fixed, hence P fixed and a single width limit. Labels are fixed independent of width (Sec. 7), recorded as amplitude order-one.
- Thm 5.6 rests on Props 5.3 and 5.5, whose proofs are only outlined ("easy modification" of App. H.6 and H.7).
- r4: since initialisation variances were tuned, the finite-width results also represent finite SP networks (footnote 42). The relu GP/NTK baselines and the linear GP/NTK limit reach 47.60, 47.82 and 41.68% on Omniglot (Table 2).
- op-10 is listed because Thm H.13(4) states that feature-learning limits are deterministic and so incompatible with Bayesian (NNGP-type) views; the paper makes no quantitative comparison with a posterior.
