one_line: "A one-parameter family of width scalings interpolating NTK and maximal-update scaling, with dNTK and ddNTK scalings that give a depth-with-width rule keeping representation learning."
summary: "The note asks that preactivations stay of order one in the hidden layers (criticality) and that every layer contributes equally to the NTK (learning-rate equivalence). Together with finite learning, this leaves a one-parameter family with $s\\in[0,1]$ that interpolates NTK scaling ($s=0$) and maximal-update scaling ($s=1$). The leading differentials of the NTK, which control kernel change during gradient descent, scale with the emergent scale $\\gamma=L/n^{1-s}$. Hence any $s<1$ keeps representation learning if depth grows as $L\\sim n^{1-s}$, and the resulting effective theories are weakly coupled for small $\\gamma$. The paper contains no numerical experiments."

- RG record: (a) Sense (b), effective theory of finite-width networks in the Roberts-Yaida-Hanin line, but the note states no renormalisation and no RG flow: those words do not occur, and the layer-to-layer recursions Eqs. (18), (23), (38), (41), (44) are used only to count powers of $n$. (b) Nothing is integrated out; the $1/n$ effective theory of the book (Ref. [19]) is imported, and the note adds the metaparameter $s$ and the scale $\gamma=L/n^{1-s}$, Eq. (46). (c) The only "flow" is exponent bookkeeping: Eq. (31) fixes $p_\ell,q_\ell,r$ from criticality (Eqs. (19)-(20)) and learning-rate equivalence (Eqs. (27)-(29)), and the leading dNTK and ddNTK scales are Eqs. (39), (42), (45); this is leading order in $\gamma$ with no loop expansion. (d) Nothing beyond leading order is computed except the power counting of higher NTK differentials for one hidden layer (Eqs. (61), (63)); maturity is derivation by power counting with no numerics, and the strongly coupled $s=1$ regime is left open (Sec. V).
- Read: the full arXiv HTML v2 (main text Secs. I-V, Appendix A); the paper has no figures, tables or experiments. The paper is a short note with no journal version, so `venue: preprint`. The addendum added in v2 records that the family coincides with the uniform parametrization of Yang and Hu (relation `same-result-as`).
- Most scalings at $s=0$ are imported from the book (Ref. [19]) and only the extra $n^{s}$ factors are counted here; the paper itself says Eq. (39) "is imprecise" (footnote 16) and points to the book for the exact statement. I did not re-derive them. r2 to r5 are therefore `phys` with grade A, and `moves_mean` is `unknown` because the note does not state how the mean prediction changes (footnote 16 only says the $y^2$ combination enters the end-of-training prediction).
- Conventions: $L$ counts all layers including the output (footnote 2), so one hidden layer is $L=2$ (footnote 18); the $L$ in $\gamma$ is just an order-one number for shallow networks. Smooth activations are needed (footnote 7, ReLU excluded). The scalings hold for the learning-rate tensor of Eqs. (34)-(35); a plain Kronecker-delta learning rate (standard parametrization) is a different point (footnote 5).
- Vocab gap: parametrisation (the interpolating family with $s\in(0,1)$ is neither NTK nor μP; wanted a value for the uniform / interpolating parametrisation; used `ntk`, `mup`, `other`).
- Vocab gap: prior_scale (readout variance $n^{-1-s}$ is between NNGP scaling at $s=0$ and mean-field scaling at $s=1$; used `nngp-scaling`, `mean-field-scaling`, `other`).
- Vocab gap: width_limit (joint width and depth scaling with $L/n^{1-s}$ fixed has no value of its own; used `inf-depth` for it and `large-width-1/N` for the weakly coupled regime, which is perturbative in $\gamma$ and so in $n^{-(1-s)}$ rather than strictly in $1/n$).
- Vocab gap: method (power counting of layer recursions in $n$ is not a listed method; used `perturbative-1/N`).
- Vocab gap: mechanism (r1 only sets scalings and describes no feature change; used type T6 with `not-analysed` because the family is about the training dynamics).
- op-09 is listed because $\gamma=L/n^{1-s}$ acts as an effective depth for gradient descent, next to the posterior effective depths in that problem. The paper is gradient-descent only and makes no statement about a posterior.
