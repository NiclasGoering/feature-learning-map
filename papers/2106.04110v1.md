summary: "The paper considers networks trained with noisy gradient descent, weight decay and square loss on a large training set, whose equilibrium is a finite-width posterior. A saddle-point treatment of the cumulant expansion of the output prior shows that the mean predictor equals Gaussian-process regression on a target shifted by a self-consistent term built from higher cumulants. For a two-layer linear CNN with a teacher this gives a scalar equation that matches trained networks far from the GP limit. The same framework shows a sharp transition in the spectrum of the hidden-weight covariance, where an outlier aligned with the teacher feature appears below a critical number of channels. A quadratic fully connected network is also treated numerically."

- Read the main text of v1 (HTML and PDF) and App. A (saddle point, covariance, validity criterion); App. H and the start of App. I for the numerics; other appendices skimmed. `venue` is `preprint` as on arXiv v1.
- Learning rule is `bayes`: the object is the Gibbs equilibrium of full-batch noisy GD with weight decay (Eq. 1), i.e. unadjusted Langevin (`sampler: ula`). The tags are R2 / `large-width-1/N` because the cumulants scale as $C^{1-r/2}$, but the saddle resums all orders and works at large $n$ and finite $C$ (EK limit).
- `closure: exact`: the full cumulant generating function enters the saddle (Eqs. 7, 14, 19); the channel law is Gaussian for the linear CNN by computation, not by assumption. At fourth order the target shift is the energy tilt $\frac12\mathrm{Cov}(K,K)vvv$; there is no log-determinant term at saddle level. It sits in the first beyond-saddle correction (A.34), estimated as $O(1/(n(\hat\delta g/\sigma^2)^2))$ (A.37).
- Eq. (16) as printed in the HTML: $\alpha=\frac{s}{\lambda+s}+\frac{(1-q)\lambda}{\lambda+s}+(\frac{q\lambda}{\lambda+s}-1)\frac{\lambda^2}{C}(\frac{\alpha}{s})^3[1-\frac\lambda C(\frac\alpha s)^2]^{-1}$, $s=\sigma^2/n$. The constant $q$ carries $O(1/n)$ EK corrections and is partly a fit parameter, so grade B. Eq. (18) and $C_{\rm crit}=473$ (Fig. 2) were not re-derived.
- Empirics (App. H): $\sigma^2=1$, learning rates $10^{-6}$ and $3\times10^{-7}$ with 10 and 30 seeds, error bars from the spread across seeds and time. `mixing: low` is the closest value; there is no autocorrelation analysis.
