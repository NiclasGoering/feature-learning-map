summary: "The paper treats an ensemble of networks at initialisation as a field theory over functions, whose action is the negative log-density of the output. Non-Gaussianity comes from finite-width $1/N$ corrections or from a small breaking of parameter independence, which survives at infinite width. A diagram prescription derived from the Edgeworth expansion computes the action from connected correlators; at order $1/N$ for one hidden layer it adds a non-local quartic term to the Gaussian action, worked out for cosine and exponential activations. Deforming the parameter density by an operator insertion engineers interactions, giving local $\\phi^4$ theory at infinite width, and a smearing layer gives cluster decomposition. Training is not studied, and a one-sentence remark on renormalisation-group flow of the $\\phi^4$ coupling carries no equation."

- RG record: Sense (c), neural-network field theory, but renormalisation enters only as one unnumbered remark at the end of Sec. 4.2, with no equation and no computation. Nothing is integrated out in the paper; the remark says that lowering the hard momentum cutoff $\Lambda$ of the engineered $\phi^4$ network (Eq. (4.27)) at fixed correlators induces a flow of the coupling $\lambda$ governed by the Callan-Symanzik equation, read as a flow of the parameter density. The computed objects are not flows: the action reconstruction is exact in Edgeworth form (Eq. (3.7)) and is computed to first order in $1/N$ (Eqs. (3.38), (3.40)) and to first order in the independence-breaking parameter $\alpha$ (Eqs. (3.41)-(3.44)), while the $\phi^4$ construction (Eqs. (4.29), (4.30)) is non-perturbative in $\lambda$ at $N=\infty$; no second-order or loop result is computed. Maturity: analytic derivation only, no numerics; RG studies of neural-network field theories are only cited (refs [47, 48] Erbin et al., [45] Erdmenger et al.), and Sec. 1.2 lists "field theory flows" and training dynamics as future work.
- Read the full v2 HTML (Secs. 1-5, Appendices A-E; reference list scanned for the related-work IDs). The paper has no numerics and no theorem environments, so every result is `phys`. The Appendix C algebra was not re-derived. The mean-free assumption (zero odd correlators) is used throughout.
- Judgement calls: `learning_rule: bayes` because the object is the prior ensemble over functions (the convention of the map's entries for 1910.00019 and 2204.01058); regime tag R2 follows r1, r2. The 1/N result is stated at leading order only: in the paper's diagram language the leading $g_4$ is a tree diagram, loops enter at $O(1/N^2)$ and above (Sec. 3.3). The Cos-net independence-breaking cumulants (Eqs. (2.54), (2.55), (B.6), (B.7)) are not listed as a result because the formulas disagree (next bullet).
- Inconsistencies in the paper: the leading bracket of $G^{(2)}_{c,\text{Cos}}$ is $\sigma_{W_0}^2(\Delta x_{12})^2$ in Eq. (2.54) but $1+\sigma_{W_0}^2(\Delta x_{12})^2$ in Eq. (B.6). The free action carries a factor 1/2 in Eq. (3.8) but not in Eqs. (1.11), (3.50), (3.53). Eqs. (1.12), (1.13), (4.9), (4.20) write $\nabla^2+m^2$ while Eq. (4.27) gives $G^{(2)}(p)=1/(p^2+m^2)$, which needs $-\nabla^2+m^2$.
