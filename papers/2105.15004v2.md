summary: "The paper takes kernel ridge regression with Gaussian design, power-law kernel eigenvalues with exponent $\\alpha$ and power-law target coefficients with source exponent $r$, and a regularisation decaying as $n^{-\\ell}$. From the asymptotic solution of the Gaussian covariate model it derives the decay exponents of the excess error in four regimes set by $\\ell$, noise and sample size. The noiseless fast rates reported in recent work and the classical noisy optimal rates are two ends of one crossover. The crossover also appears on MNIST and Fashion-MNIST with corrupted labels."
