**Problem.** Every Bayesian feature-learning theory takes in the data only through the input Gram matrix $K^0$, so hierarchy, compositionality and locality enter only through it. Gradient-descent theory has analytic sample complexities on the random hierarchy model (Cagnetta et al. 2023, $P^\star\sim n_c m^L$) and on staircase targets (Abbe et al. 2021; Abbe, Boix-Adserà & Misiakiewicz 2023); no Bayesian analogue exists.

**What would settle it.** A posterior rate function or Bayesian sample complexity computed on the random hierarchy model or a staircase target and compared with the gradient-descent results.
