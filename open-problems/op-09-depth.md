**Problem.** What a deep nonlinear μP posterior prefers at intermediate target amplitude is unknown. Only limits are understood: depth-dependent representation costs $R_L$ (Ongie & Parhi 2026), bottleneck rank (Jacot 2022, 2023), and an effective depth $LP/N$ for weakly nonlinear networks (Hanin & Zlokapa 2024).

**What would settle it.** The large-amplitude limit of the deep linear posterior compared with the representation costs of linear networks (Dai, Karzand & Srebro 2021), then the same analysis with two nonlinear hidden layers.
