**Problem.** Fixed-$P$ μP posteriors (regime R3) and proportional standard-scaling posteriors (regime R1) are both solved, but their intersection is not: μP scaling with $P\propto N$, where the extensive log-determinant and the full tilt of the single-site law compete (regime R3′). Rubin et al. 2025's multi-scale theory touches it, and deep kernel machines use it as an algorithm without a posterior claim.

**What would settle it.** The deep linear network under mean-field scaling with $P\propto N$, compared with known Wishart large-deviation results, followed by the nonlinear case.
