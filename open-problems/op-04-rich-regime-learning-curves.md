**Problem.** No closed-form test error is known for the μP (mean-field) posterior on any dataset. Adaptive-kernel learning curves (Lauditi, Bordelon & Pehlevan 2025) are numerical at $P\le10^3$; the kernel-regime theory of learning curves (Canatar, Bordelon & Pehlevan 2021; Loureiro et al. 2021) has no feature-learning counterpart; Bayes-optimal curves exist only when the teacher is drawn from the prior.

**What would settle it.** A spectral theory of the adapted kernel, or a Gaussian-equivalence result for the tilted single-site law, giving test error as a function of $P$ on structured data.
